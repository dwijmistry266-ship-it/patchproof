# Code of Conduct

PatchProof is committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

Participants should communicate with professionalism, assume good faith while verifying claims, focus criticism on code and evidence rather than people, and make room for different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and intentionally misleading reports are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
